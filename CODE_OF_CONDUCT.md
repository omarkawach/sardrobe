# Code of conduct

Sardrobe is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect privacy. Never post another person's private information, credentials, or wardrobe data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Trolling or abusive conduct is not excused as humor, technical disagreement, or criticism.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other community spaces associated with Sardrobe. It also applies when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** flow and label the report as a code-of-conduct matter. Include relevant links or screenshots, but do not collect or disclose unrelated personal information.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and will not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participation permanently. Responses will reflect the severity, impact, and pattern of the behavior. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.
